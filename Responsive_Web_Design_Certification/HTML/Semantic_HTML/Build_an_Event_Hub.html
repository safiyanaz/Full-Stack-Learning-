MET Gala

The Met Gala, formally called the Costume Institute Benefit, is the annual haute couture fundraising festival held for the benefit of the Metropolitan Museum of Art's Costume Institute on the Museum Mile of Fifth Avenue in Manhattan.

The Golden Globes are awards presented for excellence in international film and television. It is an annual award ceremony held since 1944 to honor artists, professionals, and their work.
