Unlikely Animal Friends

We’ve all heard of strange friendships in movies – think of Pumbaa the warthog and Timon the meerkat in The Lion King! This month, NAT GEO WILD tells the heartwarming stories of real-life creatures that became best buds…

The Fox and the Hound

Two dogs befriend a raccoon, a German shepherd helps raise a baby fox and a camel buddies up with a golden retriever.

Cheetah Cub, Puppy Love

This episode includes a cheetah that is friends with a rhodesian ridgeback, a veterinarian who is friends with a pig, and a couple of dalmatians that are cuddling partners with a cat.

Barnyard Buddies

This adorable barnyard episode features heartwarming stories of all kinds of different farm animals. Watch a cat that goes horseback riding on his best friend, and a chihuahua who buddies up with small dwarf goats!
